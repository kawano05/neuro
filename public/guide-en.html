Quick guide

For families, teachers and supporters. This app is for practising “pressing” with the NeuroNode switch through play. It starts with games where something always happens whenever you press.

1Before you start (once)

  • Connect NeuroNode to the iPad as a keyboard. Pressing the switch moves the games forward.
  • Leave the iPad's Settings → Accessibility → Switch Control off. Choices are made with the app's yellow highlight (nothing needs to change inside the app).
  • Sound matters in these games. Turn the iPad's volume up and turn silent mode off.

Only if you want to use the iPad's own Switch Control, turn on both the iPad's setting and the app's “Switch settings” → “Use iPad Switch Control” in the supporter settings. If only the iPad's setting is on, the yellow highlight (app) and the iPad's highlight both appear and choosing becomes hard.

4Supporter settings

Open it by touching “Supporter settings” at the top right of the game-choosing screen (it does not open with the switch, so the player cannot open it by mistake, and the yellow highlight stops on the settings screen). To go back, use “← Back to home”. Changes are saved automatically. With a keyboard, move with Tab and open or close headings with Enter / Space.

“Common settings” (the 8 shown from the start)

  • Language / 言語 … Choose the language for the games and the supporter screens.
  • Play atmosphere … Choose how lively the world is and how pressing and finishing are celebrated.
  • Read aloud … Reads the instructions and praise aloud.
  • Sound effects … Plays press sounds and applause. The cues of the games are never muted.
  • Highlight speed … Time before the highlight moves to the next item.
  • Large text … Makes the text on screen larger.
  • Hide games that need close watching … Hides “Picture match” and “Claw machine” from the home screen.
  • Every press wins … Any press counts as a win, whenever it is pressed.

Detailed settings (tap a heading to open it; any number can be open at once)

  • Switch settings … Use iPad Switch Control, Move the highlight automatically, Sound when the highlight moves, Show an on-screen “Press” button
  • Display and voice settings … High contrast, Voice volume, Voice
  • Difficulty of each game … speed, rounds and more for Picture match, Sound game, Claw machine, Fishing (also changeable from “Game settings” during play)
  • Sound credits … authors and sources of the voice and sound materials
  • Research (practice / measure) … only for research measurements. Normally leave it on “Practice”. In measured runs, the speed, rounds and cues of the games are fixed. The press timing baseline is also here.

Each group of detailed settings has a “Reset” button. After pressing it, what was reset appears just below. A greyed-out item shows the reason on its row (in measured runs, the value used for measuring is also shown).

Play atmosphere (in the common settings; choose one of four)

  • None … Pictures and press feedback. Finish with a message and a still star.
  • Simple … Small sparkles and a star ring. The background stays still.
  • Lively … The usual play. An otter and sparkle jar welcome you at the result.
  • Party … Stars collect after each success. Finish with a parade and an outfit reward.

For people sensitive to light or sound, choose “Simple” or “None”. Flashes are limited to 3 per second in every atmosphere. In measured runs, no effects are added to the games whichever atmosphere is chosen.

5Troubleshooting

Both the yellow highlight and the iPad's highlight appear
Turn the iPad's Switch Control off (see “Before you start”).
The highlight does not move
Turn on “Switch settings” → “Move the highlight automatically”. While “Switch settings” → “Use iPad Switch Control” is on, the app's highlight does not move.
The highlight is too fast or too slow
Change it with “Common settings” → “Highlight speed”.
Watching the screen is hard
Set “Switch settings” → “Sound when the highlight moves” to “Read the name” to hear the name at each move (“A small sound” plays a sound only).
The name is read at every move, and it is too much
Set “Switch settings” → “Sound when the highlight moves” to “A small sound” or “None”.
Light or motion seems too much, or the player gets overexcited
Set “Common settings” → “Play atmosphere” to “Simple” or “None”.
No sound
Check the iPad's volume and silent mode. Also turn on “Common settings” → “Sound effects”.
No voice, or the voice is quiet
Turn on “Common settings” → “Read aloud”. Change the volume with “Display and voice settings” → “Voice volume”.
A setting is greyed out
The reason is shown on that row. If it says “Fixed in measured runs”, switch “Research (practice / measure)” → “Practice / Measure” to “Practice” to change it.
I want to undo changed settings
Press “Reset” in that group of detailed settings.
Nothing happens when pressing
Check that NeuroNode is connected to the iPad (as a keyboard). If touching the screen works, the app is working.
I want to change the language
Choose it with “Common settings” → “Language / 言語”. The games and these supporter screens switch together.
